# Callina Privacy Policy

Callina does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- Settings (home currency, income, work hours, disabled sites, theme, mascot) are stored in Chrome's storage. If Chrome sync is on, Chrome syncs them to your own Google account; we never receive them.
- Uploaded mascot images and cached exchange rates stay on your device.
- Callina reads page text locally in your browser only to find prices. That text is never sent anywhere.
- The only network requests are to public exchange-rate services (cdn.jsdelivr.net, latest.currency-api.pages.dev, open.er-api.com) to download a currency table. These requests contain no information about you or the pages you visit, though those services can see your IP address as any website would.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking is used.
